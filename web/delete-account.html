Delete Account & Cloud Data

Author: Dheeraj • Application: TDLxHT

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have the full right to delete your TDLxHT account, authentication records, and all associated cloud data at any time.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the TDLxHT application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open TDLxHT.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top right.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm.

Your authentication profile and all synced tasks, habits, and focus metrics are immediately and permanently erased from the database.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request Portal

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, submit your account email below to request permanent deletion:

✓ Your deletion request has been received. If a cloud account matches this email address, all associated credentials and data will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

What Data is Purged?

  • Authentication Credentials: User ID, email, hashed credentials, and session tokens.
  • Cloud Synced Items: All tasks, subtasks, habits, categories, and streak logs.
  • Zero Data Retained: We do not retain archives, backups, or shadow profiles.
