Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Application: TDLxHT (v2.5.41) • Author: Dheeraj

1. Overview & Core Privacy Guarantee

TDLxHT is created and maintained by Dheeraj. We believe your daily tasks, lifelong habits, focus sessions, and personal notes belong solely to you.

TDLxHT is built on an offline-first architecture:

  • You do not need to create an account or provide an email to use the application.
  • Your tasks, habits, categories, reminders, and calculator outputs are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room SQLite database.
  • We bundle zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero behavioral telemetry tools.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use TDLxHT, data processing is classified as follows:

  • Local Application Data (Offline): Tasks, subtasks, habit streak history, focus timer logs, and custom category tags. This information is saved exclusively in local device storage and is never uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly sign in.
  • Optional Cloud Continuity Account: If you choose to enable multi-device sync, you may create an account using your email address and a password. This authenticates via our secure cloud service (Supabase) using industry-standard salted hashing and Row-Level Security (RLS). Only your authenticated account can read or write your synced records.
  • Diagnostics & Hardware Gestures: Hardware accelerometer readings for the "Shake to Quick Add" gesture are processed purely in real-time memory on your device to detect shake thresholds and are never logged, persisted, or transmitted.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Required

TDLxHT requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver essential productivity features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to deliver scheduled task alarms, habit reminders, and daily productivity summaries. You can toggle reminders on or off at any time in Settings.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures task due-date alarms fire accurately at the exact time you selected.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Re-registers your scheduled alarms and notifications when your device reboots.
  • VIBRATE: Delivers subtle micro-haptic feedback during button clicks, slider snaps, and milestone celebrations.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (specialUse): Powers background shake detection when you choose to enable the optional "Shake to Quick Add" setting.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data under any circumstances.

When you choose to enable cloud backup, data is transmitted strictly over TLS/HTTPS to our managed PostgreSQL infrastructure with strict access control. No third parties have access to your personal content.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have complete control over your data:

  • Immediate In-App Wipe: Go to Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account & Cloud Data to instantly purge all cloud records and local storage.
  • Web-Based Account Deletion Portal: In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, you can also request deletion at any time without having the app installed via our Account Deletion Request Portal.
  • Export & Backup: You can export your data at any time via Settings.

6. Security

All network communications are strictly enforced over HTTPS (TLS 1.3) via Android's Network Security Configuration (`cleartextTrafficPermitted="false"`). Release builds are obfuscated and hardened using R8 minification and ProGuard rules.
